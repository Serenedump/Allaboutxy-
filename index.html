The file on Xyriel

Xyriel Anne Bustamante Manabat placed third runner-up on Star Circle Kid Quest in 2009, the talent search that put her on ABS-CBN's radar as a child performer.

Her breakout came fast: a dual role as identical twins in the fantasy drama Agua Bendita (2010), then her film debut in Ang Tanging Ina Mo: Last Na 'To!, which won her Best Child Performer at both the Metro Manila Film Festival and the Star Awards.

100 Days to Heaven (2011) made her one of the country's most recognizable child stars, and in 2012 she headlined month-long specials of Wansapanataym.

After a brief turn in Wildflower (2017), she stepped away for six years, then returned in 2023 with Dirty Linen and Senior High. A stacked 2025 followed, six onscreen roles plus Pinoy Big Brother: Celebrity Collab Edition. In 2026 she took her first lead in a Netflix original, opposite longtime friend Kyle Echarri.
